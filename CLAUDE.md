# Portfolio: rules

Public site. Everything here is visible to anyone.

- **Never** include the employer's name, branding, logos, internal list/flow/site names, real people, customers, prices or part numbers. Fake data only.
- Before every commit, scan for company specifics (the private solution-map and harvest repos hold the real names to check against).
- Each new build gets a demo folder under `demos/<name>/` with its own `data/`, plus an entry in the `BUILDS` list in `index.html`.
- Pages are full HTML documents (doctype, charset, viewport) so GitHub Pages renders them in standards mode.
- Australian English, no em dashes.
